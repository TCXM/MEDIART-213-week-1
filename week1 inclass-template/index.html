MEDIART 213 / FALL 2026

My future,
a work in progress.

A few thoughts on the person I want to become and the everyday life I hope to build.

A note to my future self ↘

01 / THE BIG QUESTIONS

How will you begin to dream something no machine can dream?

I will start with small things from my own life, like a conversation with a friend or a place I miss. I want to make things that feel personal, even if they are not perfect. I do not know exactly what my future will look like yet, and I think that is okay.

If memory is built from shared stories, which dreams do you want written into the past?

I want to remember learning new skills, making friends, and finishing projects that once felt too difficult. In the future, I hope I can look back and see that I kept trying, even when I was unsure of myself.

What remains of you when all your stories are rewritten?

I think my values and the way I treat people will still be part of me. Other people may remember things differently, but I can still choose to be curious, honest, and kind.

If you seek to realize your hopes through AI, how will you ensure that it remains faithful to your will?

I will use AI to help me understand problems and explore ideas, but I will check its answers and make my own decisions. If a suggestion does not match what I want to say, I will change it instead of accepting it right away.

02 / THE LITTLE THINGS

What a good life might look like.

Not a perfect plan. Just a little more time for the things that matter.

  • People

    Which relationships will make your life meaningful and magical. How do you nurture them?

    My family and close friends will make my life meaningful. I want to stay in touch, share meals, and make time to listen when they need someone.

  • Places

    What are the aesthetics of the places that you live your life and how did they come to be that way?

    I imagine living in a small, bright room with plants, books, and a few photos. I want it to feel comfortable rather than fancy, with things I collect over time.

  • Stories

    How do you spend your time? What stories do you seek out - to tell or to listen to?

    I spend a lot of time studying and using my computer, but I also enjoy music and hanging out with friends. I like hearing about everyday experiences, especially how people deal with being a student.

  • Daily rhythm

    How will it sound? What will the rhythm of your day be?

    I hope my day starts with a quiet breakfast and some music. There will be time for classes and work, but I also want breaks and an evening that does not feel rushed.

  • Movement

    How will you move through the world?

    I want to walk or bike when I can and use public transportation for longer trips. I also hope to visit new places and learn from people whose lives are different from mine.

  • Room to grow

    How do you spend your time? What stories do you seek out, whether to share or to absorb and pass on?

    In the future, I want more time for small creative projects and learning things outside my major. I would like to share what I learn with friends and keep photos or notes of moments I might otherwise forget.
